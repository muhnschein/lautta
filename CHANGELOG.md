# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Lautta's cover no longer flickers when you come back to the home screen from another app. It keeps what it showed instead of drawing it again ([#297](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/297)).
- In the PDF viewer, after zooming in, dragging a page moves it with your finger again. Before, the view jumped to the left edge of the page as soon as you dragged ([#293](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/293)).

## 1.1.0

Servers need netvfs 2.0.0: install it before this update. Lautta 1.0.0 cannot use servers with netvfs 2.0.0, and this release cannot with netvfs 1.0.0. Without netvfs, Lautta works on the phone, memory cards and USB drives as before.

- In the PDF viewer's *Pages*, a landscape page sits at the bottom of its tile, right above its number, so documents that mix landscape and portrait pages line up ([#275](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/275)). Before, a landscape page sat at the top of its tile with its number far below.
- Videos open in the same viewer as photos. Swipe left or right to move through the folder's photos and videos in the order the folder shows them, and swipe up or down to go back. A video shows its first frame and a play button, and plays only when you tap it. Tapping the screen shows or hides the controls, including the position slider and the time. A video stops when you swipe away from it ([#277](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/277)).
- A video whose sound is in a format the phone can't decode, such as DTS, E-AC-3 or TrueHD in an MKV, still plays its picture, and the header now says *Cannot play the audio track*. Before, it said *Can't play film.mkv.*, and a video on a server was downloaded whole for nothing ([#285](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/285)).
- Reproducible builds: a commit always builds to the same RPM, byte for byte. `tools/verify-rpm.sh` checks one for you (`doc/VERIFYING.md`).
- Lautta speaks your language: it follows the phone's *Language* setting in all 39 languages Sailfish OS offers. Its launcher description and *Save to Lautta* in the share menu are translated too ([#36](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/36)).
- *Activity* is tidier. Tasks that need an answer come first, then running, waiting and paused ones; each says its state first, with a small icon for waiting, paused, conflicts and failures. Finished tasks take one line with their time on the right and only say how they ended when something went wrong, such as *2 of 5 failed*. Long titles wrap to a second line, so where a task goes stays readable. Holding a finished task offers *Show in folder* and *Remove from Activity*; before, it dimmed the list, showed nothing and left the task highlighted ([#273](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/273)).
- A PDF whose fill pattern repeats at a huge step, such as `tiling-pattern-large-steps.pdf` from the pdf.js tests, no longer closes Lautta: the page shows as it should.
- Extracting or opening a large file inside an archive no longer fills the phone's storage. Lautta checks the free space first and stops with *Not enough space* when the file would leave less than 256 MB free; it checks again while it writes. Before, an archive entry larger than the free space (such as a zip bomb) filled the phone completely.
- Keeping the name list of a server fresh takes far fewer requests with the next netvfs: Lautta asks about many folders at once, and on Nextcloud and ownCloud a branch that did not change is skipped whole. Server lists now update on Wi-Fi only, also in the background with *Update while charging*; *Settings › Search › Use mobile data* allows mobile data too; until then a server not yet listed says *Waiting for Wi-Fi*. A Nextcloud or ownCloud server without a list is asked to search itself, which answers at once instead of going folder by folder. With an older netvfs, servers are indexed as before, while Lautta is open.
- systemd unit files (such as `.service`, `.socket`, `.mount`, `.timer`, `.target`), Podman Quadlet files (`.container`, `.volume`, `.network`, `.kube`, `.image`), networkd `.link` and `.netdev` files and `.nspawn` files open in the text viewer with their keys and comments coloured, and show the config icon. Before, they opened with another app.
- When a folder, *Recents* or *Trash* is shown as a list, Lautta's cover shows a list too: each item's icon or thumbnail beside its name. In grid view the cover keeps its tiles.
- The PDF viewer has *Pages* in its pulley: all pages as small pictures, the page you are on marked. Tap one to go there, or type a page number under them. *Contents and pages* is now *Contents* and shows only when the PDF has a table of contents ([#140](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/140)).
- Search answers as you type: Lautta keeps a list of file names on this device and memory cards, and on servers you choose in *Settings › Search*. It builds the list at the lowest priority while Lautta is open, checks only folders that changed, and never reads what files contain. A search shows what the list knows at once, then corrects it for files added or removed since. With *Update while charging* on, the list also updates in the background while the phone charges above half battery. Matching ignores accents, so "cafe" finds "Café". Servers without a list are searched 16 folders deep; the *Search depth* setting is gone.
- In the PDF viewer, *Search* opens its field at the bottom of the screen, above the arrows that step through the hits, as in Documents. The pulley stays at the top edge, no longer under the search field ([#112](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/112)).
- In the PDF viewer the header with the file name stays at the top while you read, as in folders, so a pull on it opens the pulley on any page. It no longer shows the page count ([#147](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/147)).
- Search reaches the whole phone and your servers: pull down on Browse and pick *Search*. Pills under the search field choose where to look (this folder, *This device* for the phone's own storage, one memory card or server, or *Everywhere*, which adds memory cards and servers), what type of file, when it changed and whether to include hidden files. A pill's choices open like any context menu: tap the pill, then a choice, or hold the pill and slide to a choice. Results show as a list or grid, following *View options*, with the match in bold, in a section per location that says how far its search got, or that a server could not be reached. The search field takes the keyboard as soon as the page opens. Before you type, your recent searches for that place are listed: a search counts once you press Enter, open one of its results or leave *Search*, and a longer search replaces the one it grew from. The pulley at the top of the page clears them. Once a search has finished, adding a filter or typing more letters narrows its results at once, without searching again. A `*` or `?` in the text makes a pattern by itself; the separate filters page is gone ([#43](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/43)).
- PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations, documents, text, code and Markdown files each have their own icon, and so do mail, calendar and contact files, OneNote notebooks, keys and certificates, programs, fonts, disk images and CAD or 3D models. Files Lautta doesn't know show a plain page. Many more formats are recognised, among them Office macro and template files, Pages and Numbers, e-books, camera RAW photos, Photoshop files, more audio and video formats, comic archives (CBZ, CBR) and subtitles. Lautta opens what the phone can show; the rest opens with another app, such as calendar and contact files, which go to *Calendar* and *People*. Mail, PEM keys and certificates open as text. WMV, WMA, MIDI and AMR files, which the phone has no decoder for, open with another app instead of failing in the player ([#44](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/44)).
- Browse's folders on this device and in Android storage show what they hold: Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos, Public, Playlists, DCIM and Podcasts each have their own folder icon. *Save to Lautta* shows them too.
- *Trash* opens at the top of the list, with its title in view. Before, it opened scrolled down past the title. Its title now stays at the top while you scroll, items are grouped by when you deleted them, as in *Recents*, and *Trash* follows the grid or list choice in *View options*. The note at the top says how many days items are kept. Items with 7 days or less left before they are removed for good show their icon and days left in a warning colour.
- On Browse, Lautta's cover shows tilted lanes of its own icons, no longer free space and recent files. Folders, files and running tasks keep their covers.
- The phone's free space on Browse now sits right-aligned under *On this device*.
- In grid view, the first row of a folder sits a little lower, with some space between it and the header.
- In *Recents* and *Activity* the page title stays at the top while you scroll, as in folders. While you scroll, a date shows by the scroll handle on the right: *Today*, *Yesterday*, the weekday for the last week, then month and year. Drag the handle to jump through the list ([#194](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/194)).
- Sharing a file from a server offers the same apps as sharing a file on this device, such as Bluetooth. Before, the share sheet listed only apps that take any file, such as *Save to Lautta* ([#153](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/153)).
- A server folder you have open lists again by itself once Lautta is connected to netvfs again, for example after you change the account's access in *Settings*. Before, it could keep an error or the files of the old access until you left it and came back ([#235](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/235), [#172](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/172)).
- A conflict that comes up right after you tap *Start* on the plan summary opens its screen by itself, as later ones do. Before, it waited behind *Answer* in the activity bar ([#188](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/188)).
- Zips locked the old way (ZipCrypto), as many older tools still make them, now open and extract after asking for the password on the *Locked file* page. A wrong password asks again ([#167](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/167)).
- Before you allow Lautta to use network locations, a favourite or recent item on a server reads *Server › /Photos*, no longer an internal id such as *nv-account:23 › Photos* ([#107](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/107)).
- With every task paused, Lautta's cover shows how far they got, as *Activity* does, no longer 0% and an empty bar ([#124](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/124)).
- A video the phone cannot decode, such as some CAVS or 10-bit H.264 files, no longer freezes Lautta when you leave the player ([#161](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/161)).
- A server file opened from two places at once, such as *Open with* in a viewer while a share of it runs, downloads once and both get the same copy ([#155](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/155)).
- A tar file with an entry dated out of range (such as before 1901 or far in the future) no longer closes Lautta: it opens, and that entry shows no date ([#156](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/156)).
- Zip files with a file of 4 GB or more inside open and extract, and *Compress* to Zip writes such files with their right size, so other apps open the zip ([#166](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/166)).
- When you remove an account in *Settings › Accounts*, Lautta forgets its favourites, recents and settings even if you removed it right after adding it. Before, they could stay behind ([#230](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/230)).
- Markdown files with thousands of nested quotes or lists open in a moment instead of taking over a gigabyte of memory. Lists nested more than 8 deep and quotes more than 9 deep show at that depth, so their text stays on screen ([#162](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/162)).
- HEIC, HEIF, AVIF and JPEG XL files no longer open in the image viewer only to say "Can't show this image": Sailfish OS has no decoder for them. They show the image icon and open with another app, like other files Lautta can't show ([#158](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/158)).
- An empty folder says *Pull down to add*, as the pulley entry is called, and mentions paste only when something on the clipboard can be pasted there. *Paste here* on the clipboard bar now also shows in folders you opened before copying ([#192](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/192)).
- Videos ending in .ts, as TV and camera recordings do, show the video icon in folders on this device and open in the video player, no longer as code in the text viewer. TypeScript files ending in .ts still open as code. .mts and .m2ts files are videos too ([#160](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/160)).
- A compressed single file such as log.gz, dump.sql.xz, data.json.zst or a .bz2 file opens like other compressed files, with that one file inside to view or extract. It no longer fails with "not a supported archive" ([#159](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/159)).
- 7z files packed with Deflate, BZip2, PPMd or Zstandard extract now, and so does every file of a solid 7z, not only the first ([#157](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/157)).
- Small things: *More* in the image viewer shows icons, as *More actions* does; *Checksums* names its file; *Settings › Transfers* asks before operations of "Over 1000 items or 1 GB", no longer "953.7 MB" ([#214](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/214)).
- Long code lines in the text viewer wrap at their own indent, so wrapped parts line up with the code above them ([#211](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/211)).
- In the audio player, elapsed and total time sit on one line under the slider, at its two ends ([#212](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/212)).
- The image viewer's first-time hint *Swipe down to go to previous view* sits above the toolbar, no longer over Delete, Open with, Share and More ([#213](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/213)).
- Times of files inside a Zip are right when the Zip has no Unix times, as in ones made with Python; they were off by the phone's offset from UTC ([#206](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/206)).
- Copy and move tasks name the folder the items went to, not the place it is in: *Copied notes.txt to Mixed*, no longer *to Pictures* ([#207](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/207)).
- Search filters and *Task details* show folders on this device as *Pictures › Holiday*, no longer as file:// addresses ([#208](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/208), [#125](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/125)).
- Recents tiles on Browse shorten long names in the middle with "…", so the extension stays in view ([#209](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/209)).
- Menus offer *Edit favourite* for a folder or file that already is one, no longer *Add to favourites*: the folder pulley, *More actions*, the image viewer and Browse ([#210](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/210)).
- Browse shows only how much space is free on the phone and on memory cards and USB drives, as "12 GB free", no longer "12 GB free of 50 GB".
- Saving files shared to Lautta works like a folder in the app: *Save here* on top, what gets saved below it, then the folder with its path to tap your way up, or to *Locations* by Lautta's glyph. Folders open in place; files show dimmed, so you see what is there already. It opens where you saved last (Downloads the first time), *Locations* lists the last three folders you saved to, and the pulley makes a new folder. *Save here* is off where Lautta may not add files ([#95](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/95)).
- The folder header shows the path: Lautta's glyph for Browse, then the folders above, then the folder's name, all in one size on one line. Tap a folder to go straight back to it, tap the glyph to go back to Browse. When the path gets long, the farthest folders and the glyph hide behind "…"; tap it to list them, with Browse at the end. The same glyph marks *Browse page* in Settings. *Add to favourites* for the open folder moved to the pulley menu. *Edit path* and *Copy address* are gone ([#38](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/38)).
- The cover of Browse leaves as much room under two or more storage icons as above them.
- Lautta's own icons draw their lines as thin as Sailfish's, at every size and on every screen: servers, Checksums, links, the PDF lock, the lines on document glyphs and the glyphs on grid tiles. *View options* in Settings has its own list-and-grid icon ([#145](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/145)).
- A 7z file whose names are readable but whose contents are locked no longer accepts a wrong password now and then when it opens.
- Browse shows its Recents tiles only once there are three recent files to show, so a fresh install starts without a half-empty row.
- The conflict and plan summary screens are clearer ([#114](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/114)). A conflict shows both items side by side as tiles with their names and thumbnails, the choices as a list where only one can be picked, and which conflict of how many it is. The plan summary shows where items come from and go to, the number of files, the size and the free space, and lists names that need changing as old → new. It no longer asks about modification times, permissions or checksums: *Settings › Transfers* sets them. Conflicts asked about while a copy runs now show the item's name.
- Recents show thumbnails, and show as a grid when *View options* say Grid, with each day group starting its own row. Text files on Browse's Recents tiles and in the Recents grid show their first lines, as in folders ([#60](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/60)).
- A server whose identity changed now shows a clear security warning: red, with a warning icon, what the risk is, *Review identity* and *Go back*. A failed sign-in shows as a warning in the same style with *Update sign-in*. Both look the same whether you tap the server on Browse or open one of its folders ([#118](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/118), [#119](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/119)).
- Photos open faster in the image viewer, big ones most: the picture starts loading as your finger touches it, loads at screen size and first, and shows the camera's preview while it loads, or the thumbnail you tapped when the photo has none. Pictures you swiped past come back at once ([#41](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/41)).
- PDF thumbnails fill their tile with the top left of the first page, on this device and on servers: the top of a portrait page, not its middle, and the left of a landscape page, not a strip ([#193](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/193)).
- New items scroll into view and light up briefly: folders you make (also when saving shared files), pasted or moved items, files you compress, extracted files, scans and renamed items; in grid view the name and details light up too ([#113](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/113)).
- A folder listed again right after a change no longer shows as empty for a moment.
- Videos from servers stream and seek instead of downloading first ([#100](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/100)).
- Streaming videos no longer freeze the screen every second or so, and start and seek faster.
- Servers follow what you allow Lautta in netvfs: full access, read and add, read only, or add only, per server ([#104](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/104)). Folders show the level and offer only what it allows. Needs netvfs 2.0.
- WebDAV and FTPS servers work like SFTP and SMB: accounts from *Settings › Accounts* show up as locations (needs netvfs with WebDAV and FTPS).
- Lautta never takes a server address, password or one-time code any more: every server is an account in *Settings › Accounts*. *Connect to server* and its recent servers are gone. Tapping a server found nearby offers to set it up as an account. Needs netvfs 2.0.
- Markdown is nicer to read: headings in the ambience colour, check boxes for task lists, quotes with a bar, code on a tinted panel with syntax colours, and calmer tables, on the same backdrop as the text viewer. Plain `.txt` files open in the reading font, with *View source* for the line view. Big files no longer freeze the app while they open ([#50](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/50)).
- Settings is tidier: no previews in *View options* and *Browse page*, and the Settings list shows names only. *About* shows Lautta's glyph and no longer lists permissions or network access ([#117](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/117)).
- Memory cards and USB drives without a label show as "Memory card" or "USB drive" instead of a string of letters and digits ([#123](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/123)).
- Code, logs and other monospaced text use JetBrains Mono, in the text viewer, in grid previews and in Markdown code.
- Compress and Extract are simpler ([#115](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/115), [#116](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/116)). Compress asks only for a name and a format: Zip, 7z, tar.xz or tar.gz. Extract starts at once from *More actions* of a compressed file or from the pulley inside one. Results land in the same folder; one item comes out as is, several in a new folder named after the compressed file, and nothing is overwritten. Both show only where Lautta may add files.
- Zip and 7z files can have a password (AES-256): Compress asks for it twice and says when it is easy to guess. 7z hides the file names too; Zip cannot. Locked Zip and 7z files open and extract after asking for the password on the same *Locked file* page that PDFs use. The password stays in memory only while the file is open, in use by a task or on the clipboard, and is never stored ([#115](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/115), [#116](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/116)). Zips locked the old way (ZipCrypto) still don't open.
- The music player shows the cover and the title, artist, album, year and track number ([#111](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/111)). The cover comes from the file, else from a cover.jpg, folder.jpg or front.jpg beside it. Audio files show their cover as thumbnail in folders too.
- Sharing a file from a server shows "Getting …" while it downloads, then the share sheet. Before, nothing showed until the download was done, so a big file looked like the share was lost ([#126](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/126)).
- When a server goes away, the offline banner says when the folder shown was listed instead of "Showing the copy from ." A listing cut short by a lost connection shows its error instead of the offline banner ([#106](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/106)).
- *Checksums* in *More actions* of a file: SHA-256, MD5, SHA-1, SHA-512 and more (SHA-3, BLAKE2b, BLAKE3, CRC32, xxHash). Tap one to calculate it; tap the value to copy it. When a checksum list such as SHA256SUMS in the same folder names the file, Lautta says whether it matches. A checksum list itself offers *Verify files*: it checks every file the list names ([#40](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/40)).
- Browse starts with your three newest recent files as tiles; *All recents* opens the whole list, and a long press on a tile removes it from recents. The tiles replace the *Recents* row and can be switched off in *Settings › Browse page*. *On this device* now shows the phone's free space ([#169](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/169)).
- The cover shows what a tap brings back: the folder's first files as small tiles, free space and your newest recent files on Browse, the open file in a viewer, and Trash and Recents as tiles. While tasks run, the whole cover fills from the left with their progress ([#121](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/121)).
- While a photo without a camera preview opens, the thumbnail you tapped, in a folder or in Recents, fills the photo's whole shape, softly blurred, instead of a square between black bars, so the photo fades in without a jump ([#176](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/176)).
- The photo at the top of an image's *Details* shows at once instead of loading again ([#174](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/issues/174)).

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Browse, copy, move, rename and delete files on the phone, memory cards and USB drives, and on SFTP and SMB servers with netvfs ([#1](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/1)).
- Built-in PDF viewer ([#12](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/12)), with password, page count and reopen at the last page ([#14](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/14)), contents, go to page and links ([#16](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/16)), and text search ([#18](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/18)).
- Images zoom around your fingers ([#7](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/7)).
- Running tasks show in an Activity bar ([#11](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/11)).
- Grid view with 3 or 4 items per row ([#15](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/15)).
- Hide and reorder what Browse shows ([#17](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/17)).
- Selection mode is hard to enter by accident and easy to leave ([#19](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/19)).
- Item menus are plain Silica rows ([#20](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/20)).
- Five rounds of device feedback reshape Browse, folders, Trash and the viewers ([#25](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/25)).
- Text files show their first lines in the grid ([#48](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/48)).
- Servers show a server icon ([#58](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/58)).
- Folders inside an archive offer Extract all ([#64](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/64)).
- New app icon: a folder holding a page ([#91](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/91), [#93](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/93)).
- Scan documents into a PDF from a folder's Add menu ([#92](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/92)).
- The licence text ships with the app ([#97](https://github.com/muhnschein/lautta/pull/97)).
